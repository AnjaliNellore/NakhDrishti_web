AI CLASSIFICATION

Eight Nail Conditions

NakhDrishti's multiclass model was trained on thousands of fingernail images across eight categories.

01

Healthy Nail

Smooth surface and even colour, with none of the seven screened conditions visible.

Healthy
02

Nail Pitting

Small dents on the nail surface, often associated with psoriasis or eczema.

Screening
03

Nail Psoriasis

Pitting, oil-drop patches, thickening or crumbling linked to psoriasis.

Screening
04

Fungal Infection

Onychomycosis: yellow or white discolouration, thickening and brittle nails.

Screening
05

Onychogryphosis

Thick, overgrown, curved “ram's horn” nails that are hard to trim.

Screening
06

Nail Clubbing

Rounded fingertips and downward-curving nails, linked to long-term heart or lung conditions.

See a doctor
07

Blue Finger

A bluish tint that can signal poor circulation or low oxygen in the fingertips.

See a doctor
08

Acral Lentiginous Melanoma

A rare, serious skin cancer that can appear as a dark streak under or around the nail.

Seek care promptly
